About me

Why I love this work

I've always been the person friends call when a space feels unmanageable — not because I judge the mess, but because I see what's possible underneath it. There's something deeply satisfying about watching a room go from overwhelming to calm, and watching someone breathe easier in a space that finally works for them.

Clutter isn't a character flaw. It's usually just a system that never got built. My job is to help you build one — sorting through what you own, organizing it in a way that makes sense for your life, and leaving you with habits and labels that make it easy to maintain, long after I'm gone.

If your home, garage, or office has been weighing on you, I'd love to help you take it back.
